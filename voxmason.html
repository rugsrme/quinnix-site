VoxMason

A voice generation and audiobook production application. VoxMason brings together text-to-speech synthesis, narration workflows, and audio file management into a practical tool for producing spoken-word content.

Visit voxmason.com In Development

About the Project

Voice Generation for Real Workflows

VoxMason was built around a practical need: producing narrated audio from written text. What started as a simple text-to-speech workflow grew to encompass different synthesis engines, voice options, narration project management, and audiobook production tooling.

The application is designed for sustained narration work — not just generating a single clip, but managing a project through draft, revision, rendering, and export. That means handling chapter organization, tracking rendering status, managing audio output files, and maintaining consistency across a long-form project.

VoxMason also serves as a research platform for voice synthesis and voice cloning technologies, allowing experimentation with different approaches without committing to a single engine or provider.

What It Does

Key Capabilities

Text-to-Speech Generation

Convert written text to natural-sounding spoken audio. Supports multiple synthesis engines and voice configurations.

Narration Workflows

Manage narration projects from draft text to finished audio. Chapter organization, render queue, and project-level status tracking.

Audio Generation & File Management

Batch rendering, format conversion, and organized output file management. Generated audio is stored and catalogued within the project.

Audiobook Production

End-to-end support for audiobook projects: chapter management, metadata, audio consistency across a full book, and export for distribution formats.

Voice Cloning Research

Active research and experimentation with voice cloning techniques, allowing custom voice creation from audio samples. Results are mixed across different source materials.

Multi-Engine Synthesis

Support for multiple TTS engines allows side-by-side comparison and engine switching within a project. Engine availability depends on local configuration.

Development Status

Current State

VoxMason has a production website at voxmason.com. Core text-to-speech and narration workflow features are in active use. Voice cloning and multi-engine support remain areas of ongoing research and development.
